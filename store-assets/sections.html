Web App / WEB-128

Checkout button does nothing on Safari 17

Description

⚠️【Current Issue】

Tapping “Checkout” on the cart page has no effect — no navigation, no error.

🎯【Impact Scope】

All Safari 17 users (iOS & macOS) · checkout page · ~18% of orders.

📎【Attachments】

🎬 checkout-safari.mov📄 console.log

🧪【Reproduction Steps】

  1. Open the site in Safari 17 and add any item to the cart
  2. Go to the cart page and tap “Checkout”

✅【Expected Result】

User is taken to the payment step.

🍀【Test Environment】

Staging · Safari 17.4 · macOS 14.4 / iOS 17.4

🛩️【Additional Information】

Works in Chrome and Firefox. Started after release 2.8.0.
